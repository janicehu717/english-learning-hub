主题：How Do We Know We're Right?

阅读 · Why You Think You're Right, Even When You're Wrong ↗听力 · Julia Galef TED Talk ↗

英文讨论问题

1. What is the difference between wanting to be right and wanting to find out what's true?

2. Can being emotionally attached to a belief sometimes be a good thing?

3. Is it more important to challenge other people's biases or to recognize our own?

4. How can we remain open-minded without abandoning our principles?
